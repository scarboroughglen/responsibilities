**Purpose:** Reproduce, for any Scarborough Glen entity, the same document we built as
`Condo_IV_Statement_of_Income_and_Liabilities.md`. Run this **once per entity** (HOA,
Condo I, Condo II, Condo III). Do one entity at a time.

Paste the "PROMPT TO RUN" section below to Claude, after filling in the `{{...}}`
placeholders and attaching/pointing to the entity's inputs.

## 2. Chart of accounts — how to categorize the GL (reusable across entities)

The GL has columns: `Association Code, Association Name, Tran Date, Account, GL Ref #,
Description, Debit, Credit`. Aggregate by the `Account` code. For income accounts use net
**credits**; for expense accounts use net **debits** (debit − credit).

| Account prefix / code | Meaning | Statement treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `10-xxxx` | Assets (operating bank, CD, loan clearing) | **Balance sheet — exclude** from income/expense |
| `20-xxxx` | Liabilities (prepaid dues) | **Balance sheet — exclude** |
| `25-xxxx` | Loans payable | **Liabilities section** (track paydown/payoff) |
| `63-6310` | Common Charges | **Income — operating** |
| `63-6320` | HOA Dues | **Condos:** income *pass-through* (remitted to HOA). **HOA:** this is core operating income |
| `63-63xx / 63-69xx` (other) | Misc income (interest, working capital, lease, work orders) | **Income — other** |
| `70-7015` | Master Association Charges | **Condos:** expense *pass-through* (remittance to HOA). **HOA:** N/A (the HOA is the master) |
| `70-7256 / 70-7250 / 85-8530` | Loan principal/interest, loan fees | **Debt service — exclude from operating**; note separately |
| `70-7280` | Insurance (WC, crime, D&O, property) | **Operating expense** |
| `70-7400` | Admin (copies, postage, filings, management) | **Operating expense** |
| `70-7440` | State & local income tax | **Operating expense** |
| `70-7890` | Reserve/CD transfers | **Not an expense** (transfer); usually nets to $0 |
| `90-xxxx` | Capital projects (steps, window wells, roof work, sewer) | **Capital — exclude from operating**; note separately |

**Key convention (condos):** separate the **HOA-dues pass-through** (income `63-6320`
in = expense `70-7015` out) from the entity's **own operating** costs. Report both, and
base the *Operating Reserve* on the figure the reserve plan uses (for Condo IV that was the
all-in monthly incl. pass-through).

**Key convention (HOA):** the HOA has **no `70-7015` pass-through**; instead it *receives*
the condos' HOA dues as income and pays real shared-service operating costs (management,
landscaping, snow, pool, master insurance, utilities, clubhouse). Categorize those under
Operating expense.

## 3. Pull future repairs / funding data from the reserve plan workbook

Open `<Entity>_Reserve_Plan.xlsx` with openpyxl:

- **Components tab** — the component list: name (col A), Entity Allocated Cost (col D),
  Replacement Year (col E). The `TOTAL` row (col D) = **total replacement obligation**.
- **Fund Summary tab** — cell references:
  - `B6` = number of units
  - `B19` = **Fully Funded Balance (FFB)** benchmark
  - `B12` = Emergency Reserve target; `B14` = Operating Reserve floor
  - `C12`, `C13`, `C14` = Year-1 contributions (Emergency, Long-Term Maintenance, Operating)
  - `D12:D14` = starting balances allocated to each fund; `B5` = total reserve cash
- **Percent funded** = (reserve cash on hand) ÷ FFB.
- **Annual contribution required** = `C12 + C13 + C14`; per unit/month = that ÷ `B6` ÷ 12.

Cross-check the component allocations against `becht_report.md` (Falcon-vs-Becht analysis,
Appendix F unbundling: deck split, entry stoops, vinyl caulk/sealant, etc.).

## 4. Output structure (match this exactly)

Produce `<Entity>_Statement_of_Income_and_Liabilities.md` with these sections:

1. **Title block** — `# <Entity full legal name>` / `## Unaudited Statement of Income and
   Liabilities` / `*Prepared by {{PREPARER}}*` / `*In Reference to the Becht Engineering
   Capital Reserve Study (2026) and the Falcon Engineering Report (2022)*`
2. **Background** — 2–3 sentences: unaudited; based on GL for the period + Becht/Falcon as
   allocated to this entity (state unit count).
3. **Statement of Income and Cash Position** *(period; YTD, unaudited)*
   - **Income** table (operating; pass-through shown separately for condos; other)
   - **Expenses** table (pass-through remittance [condos]; insurance; admin; tax; debt
     service; capital projects)
   - **Excess of Income over Expenses**, with a note on what the surplus funded (loan
     payoff, reserve transfers) and that it is not distributable
   - **Cash Position** table (Operating / Reserve / CD → Total)
4. **Outstanding Liabilities** — loan balance or "$0 — paid in full <date>"
5. **Future Major Repairs and Replacements** — table from the reserve plan (Component /
   Remaining life (year) / Cost / Notes), ordered soonest-first, ending in **Total
   Estimated Replacement Obligation**. Include the flags that apply (see §5).
6. **Funding Gap Analysis — TWO frameworks (important):**
   - **A. What the plan requires (cash-flow basis):** reserve cash on hand; annual
     contribution required (~$/yr and ~$/unit/mo); Emergency target; Operating floor;
     Long-Term Maintenance "solvent every year"; total obligation. Add the note: *"This
     plan funds each obligation as it comes due; it does **not** require a large lump sum
     on hand today."*
   - **B. Industry health check (accrual basis — diagnostic only):** FFB benchmark;
     reserve on hand; **Percent funded** with the CAI band (<30% weak / 30–70% fair / 70%+
     strong). Add the note: *"A diagnostic of how far behind the entity fell — **not** an
     amount that must be held in cash."*
   - **Priorities:** the nearest-term items to fund first.
7. **Disclaimer** — unaudited; not GAAP; income from GL for the period; projections from
   Becht/Falcon; no assurance; subject to field verification.

## 5. Entity-specific adjustments

- **HOA (SG):** No residential buildings → the "Emergency Reserve" is framed as the
  **largest common asset** (clubhouse/pool), not a building. **No `70-7015` pass-through**;
  HOA dues received are core income and shared-service costs are real operating expense.
  Reserve components are pool, fencing, clubhouse, roadways, signage, lighting, etc.
- **Condo I (SG1):** Vinyl siding → include the **exterior caulk/sealant & trim
  maintenance** line (window-seal caulking protects the assembly behind the vinyl).
- **Condo II (SG2):** **Wood siding** → include **wood-siding replacement**, plus recurring
  **paint/stain/caulk** and (if applicable) **wood-trim** maintenance cycles.
- **Condo III (SG3):** **Wood siding + wood trim** → include siding replacement, wood-trim
  repair, and paint/caulk cycles.
- **All condos:** deck is split into **Framing & Footings (~30–50 yr) / Deck Boards
  (~18 yr) / Railings & Balusters (~12 yr, life-safety, replace ASAP if past due)**; entry
  stoops (concrete replacement + safety leveling/nosing) — both per Appendix F.

## 6. Conventions & decisions to preserve (from the Condo IV build)

- **Separate pass-through from own operating** (condos) so the income statement isn't
  dominated by the HOA remittance.
- **Fully Funded Balance is a diagnostic, not a cash requirement** — always present the
  funding gap as the two-framework A/B structure above. (This was a specific correction:
  do not imply the entity must hold the FFB today.)
- **Flag field-verification items** in the notes (e.g., chimney caps: flashing was replaced
  in the 2025 roof project but the **caps/chase covers were not** — confirm scope/count;
  entry-stoop count; placeholder unit costs).
- **Debt service and capital projects are excluded from operating** but noted separately.
- Keep figures tied to the reserve plan workbook so the statement and the workbook agree.
