I want to share my personal, plain-English interpretation of our governing documents because I believe the intent was cut-and-dry:

  * Inside the unit walls (fixtures, finishes, and anything serving only that unit): Unit Owner responsibility.
  * The building and its structure (roof, siding, framing, foundation, sealing/waterproofing, wall/slab/penetration, etc.): Condo responsibility.
  * Everything outside the building footprint falls under the HOA, including pipes, sanitary sewer/sewage and drainage lines, water lines (outside the building/under slabs), mailboxes, and other common exterior/common-area components.

To me, using this simple framework reduces confusion and helps prevent the “geo-economics” problem between the five legal entities (Condo 1, 2, 3, 4, and the HOA), where costs get shifted instead of assigned consistently.

If anyone believes a specific item is an exception, I think the cleanest approach is to cite the exact clause that overrides this framework for that specific case.
