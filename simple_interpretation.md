I want to share my personal, plain-English interpretation of our governing documents because I believe the intent was cut-and-dry:

  * Inside the unit walls (fixtures, finishes, and anything serving only that unit): Unit Owner responsibility.
  * The building and its structure (roof, siding, framing, foundation, sealing/waterproofing, wall/slab/penetration, etc.): Condo responsibility.
  * Everything outside the building footprint falls under the HOA, including pipes, sanitary sewer/sewage and drainage lines, water lines (outside the building/under slabs), mailboxes, and other common exterior/common-area components.

To me, using this simple framework reduces confusion and helps prevent the “geo-economics” problem between the five legal entities (Condo 1, 2, 3, 4, and the HOA), where costs get shifted instead of assigned consistently.

If anyone believes a specific item is an exception, I think the cleanest approach is to cite the exact clause that overrides this framework for that specific case.

**Clause citations — First Amendment to the HOA Declaration and By-Laws (recorded Feb 14, 2001):**

  * §1(d) defines the HOA's "Common Areas" as (i) the land comprising the Condominiums, (ii) common-service installations outside the Units (heat, power, light, gas, telephone, TV, water), (iii) all sewer, water and drainage pipes outside the buildings *or under the concrete slabs*, (iv) all parking areas on Condo land, (v) all walks and driveways, and (vi) all private roads, plus any pipe, wire or conduit that sits in one Unit but serves another Unit or more than one Unit. §13 and §19.3 make the HOA responsible for maintaining the Common Areas.
  * §13 leaves with the Condos "all buildings, any foundations, columns, girders, beams, supports or bearing walls of the Units, any portion of the exterior walls or walls and partitions dividing the Units from other Units, and any terraces, decks, porches and exterior stairs."
  * The one stated exception to "outside the footprint = HOA" is that terraces, decks, porches and exterior stairs stay with the Condo. Mailboxes aren't named, but they stand on HOA Common Area land (§1(d)(i)).
